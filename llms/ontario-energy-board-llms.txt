# Ontario Energy Board

> The independent regulator of Ontario's electricity and natural gas sectors — licensing and
> rate-regulating roughly 60 electricity distributors, the province's transmitters, storage and
> generation licensees, unit sub-meter providers, energy retailers and gas marketers, and Enbridge
> Gas. It sits above the wires-and-pipes layer of Canada's largest provincial energy market. It does
> not own assets, does not run the wholesale market (that is IESO) and holds no customer meter data —
> so everything it publishes is regulatory, rate and utility-performance data rather than consumption
> data.

## What actually exists

There is no developer portal, no API key, no signup, no OpenAPI published by the OEB and no terms
click-through. There are two real, anonymous, machine-consumable surfaces:

1. **OEB Open Data** — 40 datasets, 198 published files, served as XML, XLSX and GIS ZIP bundles under
   the Open Government Licence – Ontario. Whole-file downloads only; no query interface.
2. **OEB Regulatory Document Search (RDS)** — a live, undocumented JSON query surface over the Board's
   entire case record, running on Micro Focus / OpenText Content Manager WebDrawer. Add `format=json`
   to the same `Record` URLs the OEB itself links to and you get structured results with typed fields.

On consumer energy data the OEB publishes nothing, because it holds nothing. It is the **supervisor**
of Ontario's Green Button mandate (O. Reg. 633/21), not an obligated party — and it publishes the
implementation register naming 54 confirmed distributors and 6 extensions.

## Two things that will break your integration

1. **HTTP 200 does not mean your RDS query worked.** An unknown search clause, or a wildcard used with
   `=`, returns 200 with an empty `Results` array and the reason in plain English in `SearchTitle`. A
   client that checks only the status code reads "unknown search method" as "no records matched".
2. **www.oeb.ca throttles by going silent.** No 429, no Retry-After, no published limit — the host
   simply stops answering under parallel fetching (observed at ~200 requests in 20 minutes,
   2026-07-27), then recovers. Fetch Open Data serially and cache against Last-Modified.

And one that will make you read stale data: Open Data files are versioned by **filename**
(`scorecard_data_2024.xml`, `open-data-electricity-map-20260429.zip`). A refresh gets a new name while
the old URL keeps serving the old bytes. Re-resolve from the dataset page before every scheduled fetch.
